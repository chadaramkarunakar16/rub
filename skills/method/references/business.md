# Business frameworks for software studies

Short definitions the Strategist and the study report use. Explain each in plain words when it
first appears in a report.

| Concept | What it means | What to look for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ideal customer profile (ICP) | The customer who gets the most value and pays most easily | Who the homepage and case studies speak to |
| Jobs to be done | The progress a customer "hires" the product to make | The before/after story in testimonials |
| Value metric | What the price scales with (seats, usage, outcomes) | The unit on the pricing page |
| Packaging | How features are split across plans | Which feature forces the upgrade |
| Product-led growth (PLG) | The product itself acquires and converts users | Free tier, self-serve signup, in-product sharing |
| Sales-led growth | Sales teams close larger deals | "Contact sales", enterprise plan, demos |
| Growth loop | Output of one user brings in the next | Shared links, templates, invites, public content |
| Network effects | Product gets better as more people use it | Marketplaces, collaboration, shared data |
| Switching costs | Pain of leaving | Data stored inside, team habits, integrations |
| Moat | Lasting advantage competitors can't easily copy | Network effects, data, brand, distribution, ecosystem |
| Why now | The shift that made this product possible or urgent | New tech (LLMs, cheap GPUs), regulation, behaviour change |
| ARR / MRR | Annual / monthly recurring revenue | Funding news, founder interviews, filings |
| Net revenue retention (NRR) | Revenue kept and expanded from existing customers | Filings and investor letters for public companies |
| CAC and payback | Cost to win a customer and how fast it is earned back | Rarely public; mark as estimate |
| Wedge | The narrow first use case that opens a bigger market | The first feature launched, early positioning |

Good free reading: Lenny's Newsletter (growth and PLG), a16z and YC blog posts on pricing and
"why now", OpenView's PLG research, and public company investor letters.
