# Startup and funding research

## Where to look (all public)

| Source | Good for |
| --- | --- |
| YC company directory (ycombinator.com/companies), filter by batch, industry, region, "is hiring" | Lists of YC companies with one-liners, batch, team size, location |
| YC Launch posts (ycombinator.com/launches) and Hacker News "Launch HN" / "Show HN" | Founders' own problem statement, early customers, demo |
| Company website: homepage, pricing, customers/case studies, careers, changelog, blog | Customer, pricing, traction, scaling signals (hiring) |
| Funding news: TechCrunch, Axios Pro Rata, The Information (headlines), Crunchbase News, Business Insider, Inc42 / Entrackr / YourStory (India) | Round, amount, date, lead investors, quotes on why |
| Investor announcements: a16z, Sequoia, Lightspeed, Accel, Peak XV, Elevation blog/portfolio posts | The investor's own reason for backing the company |
| Founder posts and interviews: X, LinkedIn, podcasts, YC "Lightcone" and "How to Start a Startup" | Why now, insight, early growth story |
| Product Hunt, G2, Reddit, community forums | Customer voice, adoption signals |
| Public filings and investor letters (listed companies) | Revenue, retention, scale |

Use the current year in searches ("AI startup raises seed 2026"). Prefer primary sources (the company or
the investor) over aggregators. Never use paywalled content you cannot read; note it as a gap.

## Allowed values in company records

- `customer_type`: b2b, b2c, b2b2c, developer, government, marketplace
- `business_model`: subscription, usage, transaction, marketplace, services, advertising, license,
  hardware, hybrid, unknown
- `last_round.stage`: pre-seed, seed, series-a, series-b, series-c, series-d+, growth, grant, unknown

## Funding concepts (explain in plain words in reports)

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Pre-seed / seed | First money, usually to find product-market fit; seed rounds often $1-5M, larger for AI |
| Series A, B, C | Later rounds to scale something that already works; each needs stronger traction |
| SAFE | "Simple agreement for future equity": money now, shares later at the next priced round |
| Valuation cap | The maximum valuation a SAFE converts at |
| Lead investor | The investor who sets terms and usually puts in the most |
| YC deal | Y Combinator's standard investment for each company in a batch (check ycombinator.com/deal for current terms) |
| Demo Day | End of a YC batch, when companies pitch to investors |
| ARR | Annual recurring revenue; the main traction number for software |
| Traction | Evidence people want it: revenue, growth rate, retention, usage, named customers |

## Traction signals that are public and fair to record
Named customers and case studies; disclosed revenue or ARR; user counts; growth claims with a date;
hiring volume and roles (careers page); rankings (Product Hunt, G2); app-store rankings; funding size
relative to stage. Always record the date with the signal.
