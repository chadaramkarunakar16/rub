At a glance

The problem

Field sales reps spend about an hour a day writing up visits, so CRMs stay stale and managers fly blind.

The solution

Reps talk after a visit; the app turns the voice note into a structured CRM update and next-step reminders.

Who it is for

Sales teams of 10-200 reps who visit customers in person (pharma, FMCG, building materials).

What they are building

Voice-to-CRM update

Speak for 30 seconds; get a filled CRM record.

Why it matters: Removes the task reps hate most, so adoption is high.

How it works: Speech-to-text, then an LLM extracts fields into a fixed schema, then a CRM API write.

[6]

Manager digest

A daily summary of every visit.

Why it matters: Gives the buyer (the manager) a reason to pay.

How it works: Scheduled job summarises the day's structured records.

[6]

What makes it special (USP)

Works offline in poor-signal areas

Records on device and syncs later, which general note apps do not do.

Regional-language voice

Supports 8 Indian languages with mixed-language speech.

Business model

How it makes money: Per-seat subscription, sold to the sales manager.

How they got customers

  • Founder-led demos to 50 sales managers — The first 50 customers came from personal outreach.[10]
  • Short demo videos on LinkedIn — Rep-in-the-field clips showing the 30-second flow.[11]

What they do well

  • Clear buyer and clear pain
  • High rep adoption because it removes a chore
  • Local-language moat

Drawbacks and risks

  • Depends on third-party speech and LLM prices — A price rise or quality drop hits margin directly.
  • Large CRMs can copy the core feature — Distribution advantage favours incumbents if they prioritise it.
